# Logo sources

- Wix: navigation SVG from https://www.wix.com/
- Wix Studio: full black logotype from https://www.wix.com/studio/about/brand-guidelines (official downloadable logo ZIP).
- WordPress: official W mark, https://s.w.org/style/images/about/WordPress-logotype-wmark.png, listed at https://wordpress.org/about/logos/
- HighLevel: original full-color arrows from the official logo kit at https://marketing.gohighlevel.com/brandkit
- Git: https://git-scm.com/images/logos/downloads/Git-Icon-1788C.svg. Git logo by Jason Long, CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/.

Logos are stored locally, with proportions and original brand colors preserved. The remaining technology assets were retained from the original portfolio.
